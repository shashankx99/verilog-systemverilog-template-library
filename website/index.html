ABOUT THE PROJECT

Built to make RTL learning easier.

Created by Shashank Singh, an M.Tech student in VLSI Design at Thapar Institute of Engineering & Technology.

This collection brings together digital logic, RTL design, and SystemVerilog programming examples, with explanations to help you understand and remember each concept.

These are educational examples. They have not been validated through compilation, simulation, synthesis, or FPGA testing.
